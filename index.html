How do I pay?
You can pay your driver in cash (USD, EUR or EGP) at the end of the trip. If you prefer, we can send a card payment link or bank transfer details on request.

        
How will I find my driver at the airport?
Your driver meets you in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name. Before you land, we send you the driver's name, car plate and phone number on WhatsApp.

        
What happens if my flight is delayed?
Add your flight number to the booking. We track it, move the pickup to your real landing time and include the waiting time at no charge.

        
Are the prices really fixed?
Yes. We confirm one price in USD on WhatsApp before you travel. It includes fuel, tolls and parking, so nothing is added at the end.

        
Can you provide a child seat?
Child seats are available on request. Mention the age of your child in your message and we will have the right seat in the car.

        
Can I cancel or change my booking?
Message us on WhatsApp with your new plan. Changes and cancellations made at least 24 hours before pickup are free.
